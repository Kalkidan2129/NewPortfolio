
# Us Coffee Chain

## Project Overview

This project involved analyzing sales and profitability data for a coffee chain selling 13 products across 4 product types in 20 states. Using Power BI and Tableau, various measures like Sales Per Month, YOY%, and GPM% were calculated to identify underperforming products and contributing factors. The analysis provided detailed insights through drill-downs, filtering, and calculated measures to support data-driven decision making.

---

## Business Problem

The coffee chain faced challenges in identifying which products were underperforming and understanding the factors impacting profitability. Without clear insights, strategic decisions to improve sales and margins were difficult to implement. The project aimed to uncover these issues through comprehensive data analysis.

---

## Objective

- Identify underperforming products and product types affecting profitability
- Analyze sales trends and profitability metrics across markets and time
- Provide actionable insights to improve product performance and margins

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Tableau
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Excel
- Power BI Desktop
- Data Modeling
- Calculated Measures
- Filtering and Drill-down Techniques

---

## Project Workflow

- Load and understand sales data from Excel into Power BI
- Create calendar table and establish data model relationships
- Develop DAX measures for sales and profitability metrics
- Build interactive reports using filtering, drill-down, and hierarchy features
- Analyze results to identify underperforming products and factors

---

## Key Insights

- Certain coffee products consistently showed lower gross profit margins across multiple markets
- Sales trends varied significantly by product type and month, impacting overall profitability
- Year-over-year growth rates highlighted products with declining performance
- Market-specific factors contributed to underperformance in select states
