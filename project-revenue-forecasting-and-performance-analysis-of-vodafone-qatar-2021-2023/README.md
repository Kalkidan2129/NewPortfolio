# Revenue Forecasting and Performance Analysis of Vodafone Qatar (2021–2023)

## Project Overview

This project addresses the challenge of accurately predicting revenue and understanding customer behavior in the telecom industry. By analyzing extensive data from Vodafone Qatar, it empowers decision-makers to identify trends and make informed strategic choices that can enhance business performance and customer satisfaction.

The implementation focuses on leveraging advanced data analytics techniques to process and interpret large datasets efficiently. Key design decisions include the use of predictive modeling to forecast revenue trends and the integration of real-time analytics to provide immediate insights into customer behavior. This approach ensures that stakeholders receive timely and actionable information, allowing them to respond swiftly to market changes.

As a result of this project, Vodafone Qatar has seen a notable improvement in its forecasting accuracy, leading to more effective resource allocation and enhanced financial planning. The insights generated have not only optimized revenue strategies but also contributed to a deeper understanding of customer needs, ultimately driving growth.

---

## Business Problem

Telecom revenue performance is inconsistent across regions and customer segments, making it difficult for teams to identify growth opportunities and risks. Without a consolidated view of revenue, churn, and subscriber behavior, finance and strategy teams struggle to make informed decisions.

---

## Objective

- Create a unified, analytics-ready telecom dataset for accurate revenue forecasting.
- Enable performance analysis across regions and customer segments.
- Support finance and strategy teams with improved visibility into revenue drivers and churn risk.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- NexusMax
- Python
- Microsoft Fabric
- Numtra
- Power BI

---

## Project Workflow

- Ingest and clean raw telecom data using Numtra and Python.
- Store and manage the processed data within Microsoft Fabric.
- Transform and enrich the dataset to create ML-ready features.
- Develop interactive dashboards in Power BI for performance monitoring.
- Facilitate data-driven decision-making through comprehensive analysis.

---

## Key Insights

- The dataset includes key performance indicators such as ARPU, churn rate, and customer satisfaction.
- Time-based attributes allow for trend analysis and period-over-period comparisons.
- Calculated features like Revenue QoQ (%) and Subscriber QoQ (%) enable quick identification of growth patterns.
